How it works

One upload, three AI passes, a finished vertical clip with captions burned in.

01

Upload a video

Pick any long-form video from your device. MrAiBos transcribes it and finds natural pauses and silences to work with.

02

AI finds & scores the clips

Claude reads the transcript, scores candidate moments, and writes a hook, caption, and CTA for each one it picks.

03

Edit, schedule, publish

Reorder captions, translate, regenerate a variant, schedule it on the calendar, or publish straight to YouTube.
