Privacy Policy

MrAiBos is an AI-assisted tool for turning long-form video into short clips, and for generating content ideas, scripts, and images. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it — in plain language, not boilerplate.

Last updated September 11, 2026Applies to the MrAiBos mobile app & API

1Who we are

MrAiBos is a small, independently operated app. There is one team behind it, reachable at the email address at the bottom of this page. We are the "controller" of the data described below — we don't sell it, and we don't share it with data brokers or advertisers.

2Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the features you actually use.

3How we use it

  • To run the core features: transcription, AI clip scoring, caption burning, idea/script/image generation, and calendar scheduling.
  • To publish content to YouTube or TikTok only when you tap Publish — we never post on your behalf automatically.
  • To send you account emails (verification, password reset) and, if you opt in, a local notification when a job finishes.
  • To fix bugs and understand which features are actually used, in aggregate — never to build an advertising profile.

4Third-party services we use

To do its job, MrAiBos sends the minimum necessary data to a small number of specialist providers. Each only sees what it needs to perform its specific task:

Each provider processes this data under its own privacy policy and only for the purpose listed above — never to train models on your data beyond what each provider's own terms already govern, and never to serve you ads.

5Storage & security

Your account and content data live in a PostgreSQL database we operate; uploaded and rendered video files are stored on the same server. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt — we never store or can see your plaintext password. OAuth tokens for connected platforms are stored server-side and used only to make the specific API calls you request (publish, refresh analytics).

6Retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Email us (below) to request deletion of your account and everything tied to it — uploads, generated content, and connected-platform tokens are all removed; we may keep minimal records where we're legally required to.

7Your choices

  • Disconnect a platform anytime from the Menu — this revokes and deletes the stored token immediately.
  • Turn off notifications in your device's system settings; nothing else about the app changes.
  • Export or delete your data by emailing us — we'll respond within a reasonable time.

8Children

MrAiBos is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

9Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use MrAiBos after a change means you accept the update.

10Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your data: mirabboskazimjanov@gmail.com.
